# Docquier et al. (2022): Arctic Variables Guide

This is a quick summary of the variables used by Docquier to study Arctic causality. 

## Variables Used for Causality
Docquier uses the SMHI-LENS (EC-Earth3) model to study causality between the following 7 variables:
1. **Arctic Sea-Ice Area (SIA)**
2. **Arctic Sea-Ice Volume (SIV)**
3. **Arctic Near-Surface Air Temperature (T2m)** 
4. **Arctic Sea-Surface Temperature (SST)**
5. **Total Arctic Ocean Heat Transport (OHTA)**
6. **Ocean Heat Transport at 70°N (OHT70N)**
7. **Atmospheric Heat Transport at 70°N (AHT70N)**
8. **Arctic Oscillation Index (AOI)**
